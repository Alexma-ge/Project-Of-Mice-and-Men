# Project Of Mice and Men

### *"Seeing what was read: How non-visual prior knowledge shapes neural synchrony during naturalistic movie watching"*


Prior knowledge shapes how sensory information is processed and interpreted, yet the extent to which prior knowledge and expectations influence neural processing remains incompletely understood. The present study investigated whether non-visual prior knowledge about the narrative acquired through reading influences neural synchrony during naturalistic movie watching. Participants who had read John Steinbecks' “Of Mice and Men” (1937) were compared with participants who had no prior exposure to the narrative while both groups watched the movie adaptation of the novel during functional magnetic resonance imaging.
